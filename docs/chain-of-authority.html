Escalation Protocol

Escalation Path

  • 1
    If your direct supervisor is unavailable, go one level up in the relevant track (Creative or Production).
  • 2
    For production-critical issues — delivery at risk, pipeline down — contact the VFX Supervisor AND Producer simultaneously. Do not wait for one to respond before reaching the other.
  • 3
    For IT emergencies, submit a Support Ticket with High priority via the Hub before or alongside any other contact.
External Vendor Protocol

GPL Tech Authorization

All requests to GPL Tech (our IT provider) must be initiated by or approved by a VFX Supervisor or Producer. Do not contact GPL Tech directly for infrastructure changes without internal authorization.

Submit your request via a Support Ticket at the Hub. The ticket will be routed to GPL Tech once internal authorization is confirmed. Unauthorized direct contact may result in requests being rejected or delayed.
